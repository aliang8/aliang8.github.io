Introduction

In this lesson you will learn ~거니, a way to express an opinion you have largely settled on as true. It describes what you figured must be the case, even though it has not been confirmed.

1

~거니: Confident Assumption

Attach ~거니 to verb and adjective stems to express a guess the speaker has settled on as likely to be true. It is often used to explain what the speaker assumed or expected. ~거니 is commonly attached to ~겠 to show that the situation is somewhat of a guess. For example:

여자 친구가 그렇게 화가 났어도 내일이면 연락이 오겠거니 생각했어요
Even though my girlfriend was that angry, I figured she’d contact me by tomorrow

Here the speaker did not know for sure that this would happen, but was confident enough to settle on it mentally. In a sense, the speaker took the assumption for granted.

As in the example above, ~거니 usually translates to something like:

I figured that…

Other common translations of these sentences:

I was confident that…
I assumed that…

In Lesson 202 you learned ~(으)려니, which also marks a guess from the speaker. ~(으)려니 presents something as an assumption the speaker has made; ~거니 suggests the speaker has settled on that assumption as likely to be true.

Compare these two sentences and their English translations:

그가 잘 하려니 생각했어요
I assumed he’d do well (but maybe that was naïve)
그가 잘 하겠거니 생각했어요
I was confident he’d do well

You can also attach ~거니 to ~았/었~, to show that the speaker had settled on the assumption that something had already happened. For example:

회의는 비공식적이었으니 조용히 끝났거니 여겼어요
I assumed it had ended quietly since it was unofficial
회의가 비공식적이었으니 곧 끝나겠거니 생각했어요
Since the meeting was unofficial, I figured it would end quickly

Here are many examples using the vocabulary from this lesson:

퇴각 명령은 이미 내려졌거니 생각했어요
I thought the retreat order had already been given
섭외는 다 끝났거니 생각했는데 아니었어요
I thought casting had already been wrapped up, but it wasn’t
한동안 연락이 없어서 다 정리됐거니 여겼어요
Since there was no contact for a while, I assumed everything had already been settled
섭외가 끝났으니 곧 촬영을 시작하겠거니 했어요
Since casting was done, I thought filming would begin soon
자료가 창고에 고스란히 남아 있겠거니 생각했어요
I assumed the materials would still be intact in the storage room
일설에 따르면 그 사람이 이미 사임했겠거니 생각했어요
According to one account, I assumed that person had already resigned
창작자가 직접 만든 작품이니 독특하겠거니 생각했어요
Since the creator made the work himself, I assumed it would be unique
학생이 이미 숙달했으니 문제없이 해내겠거니 믿었어요
Since the student had already mastered it, I was confident they’d pull it off
곡조가 이렇게 독특하니 금방 주목받겠거니 생각했어요
With such a unique melody, I figured it would get attention quickly
로켓을 이미 띄웠으니 곧 궤도에 도달하겠거니 생각했어요
Since they had already launched the rocket, I assumed it would reach orbit soon
두 국가가 연합했으니 쉽게 무너지지 않겠거니 생각했어요
Since the two countries had formed an alliance, I assumed it wouldn’t collapse easily
기존 방침은 이미 공유됐거니 하고 따로 설명하지 않았어요
I assumed the existing policy had already been shared and didn’t explain it again
옷차림이 꼬장꼬장하니 준비를 제대로 안 했겠거니 생각했어요
Since his clothes looked messy, I assumed he hadn’t prepared properly
두 사람의 관계가 각별하니 서로를 끝까지 돕겠거니 생각했어요
Since their relationship was special, I assumed they would help each other to the end
회의장에서 그저 형식적인 이야기만 오가겠거니 생각했는데 아니었어요
