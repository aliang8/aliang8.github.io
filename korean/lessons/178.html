Introduction

In this lesson you will see how ~구요 can replace ~고 in the right situation.

1

~구요 instead of~고

In Lesson 17 you saw ~고 used to say that one action happens and then another follows. For example:

저는 자고 한국어를 공부했어요
I slept then studied Korean

You also saw ~고 connect two clauses with a similar idea. For example:

그 사람은 착하고 똑똑해요
That person is kind and smart

A sentence can end with ~고. In a polite situation, add ~요 to get ~고요. Ending a sentence this way tells the listener that more information is coming. For example:

저는 대학교에서 공부하고요. 지금 3학년이에요.
I study at university. And I’m in my third year.

Some Korean accents pronounce ~고 closer to 구, and people sometimes say it that way colloquially too. Couples even use ~구 instead of ~고 just to be cute.

In everyday speech (and even some writing) ~구요 is much more common than ~고요. ~구요 softens the tone and is less direct than ~고요. Technically ~구요 is just the spoken form of ~고요, but in practice it carries a friendly, sympathetic feel. Use it when gently adding detail rather than listing facts. For example:

저는 이슬기이구요, 한국에서 산 지 20년 되었어요

Here ~구요 attached to 이다 adds a friendly, spoken tone, as if the speaker is easing into the second detail. Another example:

그 남자는 돈에 혈안이 되어 밤낮없이 일했구요, 결국에는 건강이 크게 나빠졌어요
That man was obsessed with money and worked day and night, and in the end, his health greatly deteriorated

Here ~구요 makes the list of events feel softer and more flowing, like telling a story rather than reciting a cold sequence of actions.

Speakers often want to add an idea to a situation already being discussed. This is another job of ~고, so it is another common place to find ~구요. For example:

그 사람은 형편도 안 좋은데 가족까지 떠났대요. 게다가 주변 사람들한테 신망도 잃었구요.
His circumstances are already bad, and on top of that, he’s lost the trust of those around him, too.

Here the speaker uses ~구요 to show that the statement is part of a continuing context: a follow-up that builds on what came before. This use of ~구요 is especially natural in spoken Korean. More examples:

처음에는 말이 그럴듯하길래 믿었죠. 나중에 알고 보니 완전히 모순되었구요.
At first his words seemed plausible, so I believed him. Later I found out they completely contradicted each other too.
