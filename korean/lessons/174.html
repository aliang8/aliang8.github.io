Introduction

In this lesson you will learn a way to make your responses to praise or flattery sound more humble.

1

Downplaying praise or flattery:~기는(요)

When you receive praise or a flattering compliment, you often want to downplay it to sound humble. If somebody compliments your Korean, you might answer “thank you, but I still have a lot to learn.” Answering “yes, I know, my Korean is very good” would sound arrogant.

Attach ~기는(요) to the verb or adjective somebody used to compliment you, and you downplay the compliment and sound humble. For example:

Conversation Audio
Person 1: 콧등이 진짜 높고 예뻐요!
Person 2: 높기는요. 그냥 조명 때문에 그렇게 보이는 거예요.

Person 1 thinks Person 2’s nose is high and pretty. “높기는요” is hard to translate, since it is really a feeling of trying to be humble. In this lesson it is translated as if Person 2 is asking a question back, almost in disbelief. For example:

Person 1: The bridge of your nose is really high and pretty!
Person 2: High? It’s just the lighting that makes it look that way.
