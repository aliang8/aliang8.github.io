Introduction

In this lesson you will learn about the syllable 성. The main goal is its meaning when it indicates a range of some quality, but we will cover some of its other meanings as well. We will look at these meanings of 성:

성
性 = Character or a range of some quality
성
成 = Completion
성

Let’s get started!

1

성 (性) = Character or a Range of Some Quality

You do not need to focus on the Hanja characters for each meaning of 성, but the lesson is organized by them. The first meaning comes from the Hanja character 性.

You will most often see this 성 in a noun that refers to the characteristic or nature of something. It appears often in words about a person’s (or thing’s) character or identity. You have already seen this 성 in 성격 (one’s personality). For example:

저는 외모보다 성격이 더 중요하다고 생각해요
I think personality is more important than looks

Here are many words that use this 성:

individuality, personality

성 Used with the Noun Form of~하다 Adjectives

Added to the noun form of a –하다 adjective (or sometimes a verb), the new noun usually refers to the range or degree of that quality. Here are a couple of examples with words from earlier lessons:

가능하다
to be possible
가능성
possibility

가능성 is the range or degree of something being possible, its possibility. In a sentence, the speaker usually says where in that range something falls. For example:

내일 비가 올 가능성이 높아요
There is a high possibility it will rain tomorrow
아빠가 다시 돌아올 가능성이 없어요
There is no possibility dad will return again

The speaker can also refer to the range itself. For example:

우리가 내일 비가 올 가능성을 알아야 돼요
We need to know the possibility of it raining tomorrow

Here are more examples of –성 after a noun that also has a –하다 form. In some cases the noun left after removing –하다 is awkward on its own, so the -성 version is more common than the non-성 version:

Another example from a previous lesson is:

중요하다
to be important
중요성
importance
이 문제의 중요성을 알리기 위해 영상을 만들었어요
In order to inform you of the importance of this problem, I made a video

The word 전염성 shows nicely what –성 does. 전염성 refers to how contagious something is, whether high or low. English has no comfortable word for this (contagiousness?). One nice thing about knowing more than one language is that you can pick the word that fits a thought best, and 전염성 is a thought that is easier to express in Korean than in English.

성 Used with nouns that feel like adjectives but don’t have a~하다 adjective form

Here is a list of nouns 성 is commonly attached to. These nouns do not have a –하다 form (or at least not a common one):

산성
acidity
지성
intelligence
적성
aptitude
관성
inertia
감성
sensitivity
극성
polarity
독성
toxicity
효율성
effectiveness
사회성
sociability

Because these words have no ~하다 form, there is no natural adjective for them. Compare:

중요성 means “importance.” If the importance is high, something is “important,” which in Korean is “중요하다.”

위험성 means “dangerousness.” If the dangerousness is high, something is “dangerous,” which in Korean is “위험하다.”

You cannot do this with the words in the list above. These are not adjectives describing those properties:

Some of these are real words, but with an entirely different meaning.

산하다
지하다
적하다
관하다
감하다
극하다
독하다
효율하다
사회하다

Although these –성 words have no natural adjective form in Korean, most have a perfect one in English. For example:

If something has a high acidity, it is acidic

If something has a high sensitivity, it is sensitive

If something has a high toxicity, it is toxic

If something has a high efficiency, it is efficient

In Korean, you cannot use 산하다, 감하다, 독하다 and 효율하다 to mean acidic, sensitive, toxic and efficient. Instead, with these words (and the others listed with them) you use other adjectives to describe the property. For example:

산성이 높은 비를 산성비라고 해요
Rain with a high acidity is called “acid rain”
내 아이를 지성이 높은 아이로 키우고 싶어
I want to raise my child as a child with high intelligence
적성이 맞는 일을 찾는 게 제 목표예요
It is my goal to find a job that is right for me

Here is another Korean idea that is hard to translate. 적성이 맞다 describes a job that perfectly suits one person’s interests and abilities.

관성이 큰 물체는 움직임을 멈추는 게 어려워요
Stopping an object with a high (a lot of) momentum is difficult
저는 다른 사람들보다 감성이 더 섬세해요
I have a more delicate sensitivity than other people
몇몇 뱀은 독성이 강해서 물리면 죽을 수도 있어요
Some snakes have a high toxicity (are highly toxic), so if bitten, a person could even die
가정제품을 살 때는 효율성이 좋은 제품을 사야 돼요
When you buy appliances, you should buy products with a good efficiency (efficient products)
물은 극성이 있는 분자예요
Water is a molecule with polarity (a polar molecule)

Another common noun similar to those above is 참을성.

참을성 means “patience.” If one’s patience is high, he or she is “patient.” Korean has no common single word for “patient,” so, as with the words above, other adjectives are used with 참을성 to say that someone is patient. For example:

그 사람은 참을성이 부족해서 못 기다렸어요
That person is not patient (lacks patience), so he could not wait

성 (誠) = Loyalty, Effort, Devotion

정성을 다해서 이 음식을 만들었어요
I gave it my best when I made this food

충성
loyalty, allegiance
나라에 충성을 다해 열심히 근무하겠습니다
I pledge my total loyalty to work diligently for the country
성의
sincerity
무엇보다도 중요한 것은 성의를 보여주는 것이에요
Above all else it’s important to show sincerity
성실하다
to be faithful
그 사람은 매우 성실하기 때문에 모든 사람이 그를 좋아해요
That person is very faithful, so everybody likes him
