Introduction

In this lesson you will learn ~(으)로써, which looks a little like ~(으)로서 from the previous lesson. Korean people often have trouble remembering which is which; this lesson makes sure you won’t.

1

~(으)로써: From what ingredient, or by what means or tool

In Lesson 12, you first met ~(으)로 and some of its uses. Two of the most common uses of ~(으)로 are:

To indicate from what ingredient something is made:

그 회사는 금속으로 철사를 만들어요
That company makes wires from metal

To indicate by what means or tool an action occurs:

저는 망치로 벽에 못을 박았어요
I put the nail in the wall using a hammer

In both situations you can use ~(으)로써 instead of ~(으)로. That is, ~(으)로써 can indicate what ingredient something is made from, or by what means or tool an action occurs. For example:

그 회사는 금속으로써 철사를 만들어요
저는 망치로써 벽에 못을 박았어요

There must be some difference, right? There is. ~(으)로써 is more stressed than ~(으)로. When the thing it attaches to is obvious, ~(으)로 is the natural choice.

Of course the wires are made of metal. What else would they be made of?
Of course the nail was put in using a hammer. What else would you use?

~(으)로써 fits better when the ingredient, tool or method is not obvious or not expected. So instead of making wire from metal and hammering a nail with a hammer, these sentences would suit it better:

To indicate from what ingredient something is made:

요즘의 철사는 금속이 아닌 유리로써 만들어져요
Wires these days are made out of glass, not metal

To indicate by what means or tool an action occurs:

저는 굵은 나무로써 벽에 못을 박았어요
I put the nail in the wall using a thick piece of wood

Here are more examples of ~(으)로써 indicating the ingredient something is made from. Given the nature of ~(으)로써, it is easy to build these sentences with 만들다 or 만들어지다 as the verb, so some other verbs are used here too for variety.

낙엽으로써 복면을 만들었다
I made a mask using fallen leaves
커피는 볶은 콩으로써 만들어진다
Coffee is made using roasted beans
이곳에서는 한지로써 초청장을 인쇄한다
Here, we print invitations using Korean paper
우리는 버려진 나무로써 울타리를 지었어요
We built a fence using discarded/thrown out wood
찌개를 맛있게 하려면 육수를 양파껍질로써 내야 돼요
If you want to make jigae right, you should make the broth using onion peels
대부분의 마트에서 파는 소시지는 좋지 않은 고기로써 제조된다
Most of the sausage you buy at stores (marts) are made from bad meet

Here are more examples of ~(으)로써 indicating by what means or tool an action occurs:

포장지로써 한지를 사용하기도 한다
You can use Korean paper for packing paper, too
잘 익은 사과는 설탕으로써 사용이 가능하다
It is possible to use really ripe apples as sugar
인간은 1000년 전에 식기로써 돌을 사용했다
Humans used rocks as plates 1000 years ago
우리 회사는 광고로써 대부분의 수입을 올린다
Our company earns most of its income from advertising

~(으)로써 itself is not hard to understand or apply. Many Koreans call it difficult only because they confuse ~(으)로서 and ~(으)로써. After these two lessons, that should never be a problem for you.
