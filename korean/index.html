Korean, day by day 한국어

A self-paced schedule through every free lesson on How to Study Korean: Hangul first, then 203 grammar lessons from Unit 1 to Unit 9, with review days built in. Progress is saved in this browser.

How each day works

  1. Read the lesson once without stopping to memorise. HTSK lessons are long (about 4,000 words); the first pass is for shape, not detail.
  2. Vocabulary into a deck. Add the day's words to the HTSK Anki decks or your own. Do your Anki reviews every day, including rest days. This is the ten minutes that matters most.
  3. Second pass on the grammar, then write five sentences of your own using the new pattern. Say them out loud.
  4. Spaced review. Each lesson day also points back at the vocab from three and seven lessons ago.
  5. Mini-test after every block of eight or nine lessons, a unit test after every 25, and one consolidation day to write and speak before moving on.

Lessons that are unusually long or vocab-heavy are split over two days. Lesson 134 (the Konglish guide, 400+ words) is a skim, not a memorisation task. If a day takes you well over its estimate, slow the pace instead of skipping: the later units assume the earlier ones are solid.
